    How to use it

    1. Point it at the fight and press START. Hold the phone the way you want to film before you press it.
    2. Tap the picture whenever you see something worth a second look. It keeps filming, and you can mark as often as you like.
    3. Press REVIEW when the fight is stopped.
    4. Drag across the picture to move through the moment. The numbers switch between your marks. ✎ annotates the mark on screen: tap the picture where each hit landed — choose Red or Blue for each, drag to find the moment, Undo takes the last one back, and Done when you have them all. A dot on a number means that mark has hits, and the saved video shows each hit as a ring. BACK keeps this exchange and goes straight back to filming the next.
    5. When the bout is over, press END. Every exchange is listed with its marks, and the ones you annotated are already ticked (all of them, if you annotated none). Tick or untick any you like — the one you last touched plays behind the list, at double speed — ✎ beside a mark annotates it from there; and SAVE makes one short video of the whole bout, with a quick fade between each moment, and hands it to your phone. It records by playing them through, so it takes as long as they last.

    Turn the phone before you start

    The recording keeps the shape it had the moment you pressed START. Turning the phone while filming will not change it — the footage just comes back the wrong way round.

    Ending a bout loses it

    Nothing is kept once a bout ends. On the summary, BACK carries on the same bout, and Discard throws all of it away, asking first. Once a video has been saved, New bout starts the next one.
